Sunshine City

Harare Hustle · starring Ngwere

Play as Ngwere on the real street map of central Harare: drive kombis and mushikashika, sell at the musika, respect the robots and speed cameras (or pay), run ambulance calls, serve on national duty — and take down the tout boss taxing the whole city.

Graphics

Keyboard — W A S D / arrows: drive & walk · Shift: run · Space: handbrake · F: enter / exit vehicle · E: action · J: start a job in a kombi, taxi, e-hailing car or mushikashika · H: horn · R: radio · M: map · P: pause
Touch — left stick steers and accelerates; buttons on the right.
Earn — story missions (yellow S) · vending: buy crates at Mbare Musika, sell at orange spots · fares · ZRP, Army, City Council and Ambulance duty (blue, green, teal, pink markers).
Rules — robots (traffic lights) and speed cameras fine you: 60 km/h limit at cameras, stop on red. The coloured dot under your speed shows the next robot.
Street talk — listen for ma hwindi: “Town here”, “Zviriko”, “Bho here”, “Madiii”, “Safe here”…
New — walk into yellow Flat / Shop door labels near Copacabana & First Street · [E] enter/exit · in a car press V / CAM for cockpit view.
Zimbabwe drives on the left. Characters, artists and story events are fictional. Streets and building footprints: map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
